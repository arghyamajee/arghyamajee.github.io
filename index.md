I'm a scientist in the [Theory of Polymers](https://www.ipfdd.de/en/research/division-theory-of-polymers/) division at the [Leibniz Institute of Polymer Research Dresden](https://www.ipfdd.de/en/home). My research focuses on understanding soft-matter and biomolecular systems and encompasses two complementary strands. 

The first is physics-based modelling across two scales. At the mesoscopic level, I use phenomenological continuum models to explore how microscopic interactions give rise to collective behaviour. At the molecular level, I employ molecular dynamics simulations to investigate the underlying interactions and mechanisms. For more details on my past and current research, please navigate to the 'Research' tab.

The second is machine learning and data-driven modelling. Building on my background in nonlinear optimisation, I apply machine learning and deep learning to soft-matter and biomolecular systems. Current projects include predicting protein sequestration by hydrogels using neural networks trained on protein structural descriptors and experimental data, and investigating nucleation in undercooled polymer melts using CNNs applied to molecular dynamics simulation data.

If any of this resonates with your interests, I’d be glad to connect.
